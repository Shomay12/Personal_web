BACKGROUND

About
Me

AI & ML Systems Transformers & Attention Representation Learning RAG & Agents

I am a BCA student at Amity University, Noida, focused on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and intelligent software systems.

My approach combines theoretical understanding with practical engineering. Rather than treating machine learning models as black boxes, I focus on understanding the mechanisms behind them—from optimization and representation learning to attention mechanisms, Transformers, retrieval systems, and modern generative AI architectures.

I build software and AI systems to turn these concepts into practical implementations. My work spans machine learning experimentation, NLP, automation, backend engineering, API-driven systems, and AI-powered applications.

My current technical direction is centered around modern AI systems, including Retrieval-Augmented Generation, model fine-tuning, AI agents, and the engineering challenges involved in building reliable intelligent applications.

Long term, I want to work at the intersection of AI research and systems engineering, where understanding fundamental machine learning concepts can lead to better and more capable intelligent systems.

TECHNICAL DIRECTION

What I’m
Exploring

Modern Deep Learning

Studying neural architectures beyond conventional machine learning, with emphasis on representation learning, optimization, sequence modeling, and architectures capable of learning complex patterns from large datasets.

Transformers & Attention

Understanding self-attention, multi-head attention, positional representations, encoder-decoder architectures, and the architectural ideas that enabled modern language models.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Exploring how retrieval systems, embeddings, vector search, and language models can be combined to ground generated responses in external knowledge.

Model Fine-Tuning

Studying techniques for adapting pretrained models to specialized tasks and datasets while balancing performance, compute requirements, and generalization.

AI Agents

Exploring systems where language models can reason over tasks, use tools, interact with external systems, maintain context, and execute multi-step workflows.

AI Systems

Interested in the engineering layer behind intelligent applications: model serving, APIs, retrieval pipelines, automation, infrastructure, evaluation, and system reliability.
