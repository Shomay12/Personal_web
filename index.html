I am a BCA student at Amity University, Noida, focused on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and intelligent software systems.

My approach combines theoretical understanding with practical engineering. Rather than treating machine learning models as black boxes, I focus on understanding the mechanisms behind them—from optimization and representation learning to attention mechanisms, Transformers, retrieval systems, and modern generative AI architectures.

I build software and AI systems to turn these concepts into practical implementations. My work spans machine learning experimentation, NLP, automation, backend engineering, API-driven systems, and AI-powered applications.

My current technical direction is centered around modern AI systems, including Retrieval-Augmented Generation, model fine-tuning, AI agents, and the engineering challenges involved in building reliable intelligent applications.

Long term, I want to work at the intersection of AI research and systems engineering, where understanding fundamental machine learning concepts can lead to better and more capable intelligent systems.
